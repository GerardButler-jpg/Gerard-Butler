Greenland 2: Migration, a father on the move

Two character posters, one tagline, and a clear signal that the Garrity family is leaving familiar ground behind.

Film · 17 October 2026

The posters

The campaign for Greenland 2: Migration gives each Garrity a close-up portrait and the same line: "Hope is uncharted territory." Gerard Butler appears as John Garrity, eyes raised toward streaks of burning debris, with a hazy city skyline behind him. Roman Griffin Davis appears as Nathan Garrity, also looking skyward, with a cliff edge and a rope bridge in the distance.

Reading the imagery

  • City versus wilderness. John's poster keeps a skyline in view. Nathan's shows rough terrain and a bridge, which suggests the family is moving from built places into open country.
  • The sky as threat. Both portraits share the falling fragments, so danger comes from above in every frame.
  • Father and son as a pair. Both look up and away from the camera, which frames them as facing the same problem from different ages.

Release

The film opened in theaters on 9 January 2026, distributed by Lionsgate, with STX Films credited on the posters. It follows Greenland (2020), where John Garrity first protected his family through a global catastrophe.
