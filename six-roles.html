Six roles that show his range

The action star label sticks, but the filmography is wider than the posters suggest.

Features · 17 October 2026

King Leonidas, 300 (2007)

The role that made the name. Leonidas is all volume and conviction, and the film's stylized look gave Butler a heroic scale few action leads get.

The Phantom, The Phantom of the Opera (2004)

A musical, a mask and a romantic lead. It proved early that he could sing on screen and play longing as well as fury.

Stoick the Vast, How to Train Your Dragon (2010)

A voice role as a Viking chief, delivered with his own Scottish accent. It introduced him to a family audience and has kept him in the franchise.

Mike Banning, Olympus Has Fallen (2013)

A disgraced presidential bodyguard given a second chance. The role grew into a three-film series with London Has Fallen and Angel Has Fallen.

Brodie Torrance, Plane (2023)

A commercial pilot forced to land in hostile territory. It is a leaner, survival-driven film that suits his physical, no-nonsense style.

Mike Chadway, The Ugly Truth (2009)

A brash TV host in a romantic comedy. Charm and timing carry the part, and it shows the lighter side behind the action roles.
